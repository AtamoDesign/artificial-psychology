# Code of Conduct

This project is a research and engineering community. We want it to be welcoming to people of every background and experience level.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about the work, not the person.
- Credit others' ideas and contributions.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or sustained disruption.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer through GitHub. The maintainer may remove comments, commits or contributors that violate this code. Reports are kept confidential.
